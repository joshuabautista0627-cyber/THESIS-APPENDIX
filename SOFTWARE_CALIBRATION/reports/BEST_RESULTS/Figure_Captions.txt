01_best_localization_9x9_normalized
Localization confusion matrix for Tuned Logistic Regression, the evaluated model with the highest equal-recording accuracy. Rows are actual classes and columns predicted classes. Each frame has weight 1 divided by the evaluated frames in its recording; each actual-class row is then normalized to 100%. Six complete TEST groups were held out, covering 7,154 frames and 54 manual recordings. Model-family ranking is retrospective; these are held-out fold predictions.

05_best_models_taxel_heatmaps
Held-out localization recall for tuned Logistic Regression and positive-contact recall for tuned RBF SVC, mapped to the saved nine taxel ROI rectangles in oriented camera pixel coordinates. Each taxel value averages six recording recalls. No-contact specificity is evaluated separately in the contact matrix.

06_light_response_distribution
Optical response-distribution matrix, distinct from a classifier confusion matrix. For each taxel, D is the ROI mean positive baseline-subtracted V. Its reference floor is the median of no-contact recording medians outside the corresponding held-out group. E=max(D-floor,0). For each recording, average E_j over all eligible frames, then divide each taxel mean by the sum of the nine means and multiply by 100. Average these normalized recording profiles equally across the six recordings of each pressed taxel. All 7,154 frames are retained, including 1,153 frames with zero total excess. Every recording has a nonzero mean-response denominator. Off-diagonal response is an exploratory cross-talk/spread proxy, not calibrated optical power leakage. Acquisition CSVs are joined exactly to the held-out frame IDs.

07_light_metrics_by_taxel
Four optical diagnostics from original manual acquisition CSVs: mean target excess positive ΔV after a fold-specific no-contact floor; RMS of within-recording temporal SD of mean positive ΔV in 11 no-contact recordings; pressed-taxel active area at the saved ΔV threshold of 10; and mean excess-response/reference-noise-SD ratio. Positive values are averaged within each press recording and then over six recordings per taxel. No-contact noise displayed uses all 11 control recordings descriptively, while each press ratio uses only control recordings outside its held-out group. These are camera-based diagnostics, not calibrated lux, mechanical sensitivity, or photometric SNR.

08_light_force_correlation
Spearman rank correlation between the target taxel mean positive ΔV recorded at acquisition and the lag-aligned reference force retained in the held-out prediction table. Each cell is one press recording in the 0.05–3 N interval. No new lag, signal threshold, or fitted regression was optimized. The fixed -1 to +1 color scale retains negative associations. These within-recording descriptive correlations are not independent-frame significance tests and do not establish linearity or causal sensitivity.
